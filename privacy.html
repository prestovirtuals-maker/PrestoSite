Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Who we are

Presto Virtual Assistance Services ("Presto", "we") is a virtual assistance business in Mississauga, Ontario, serving clients in Canada and the USA. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Should you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, contact Francine at prestovirtuals@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and do the work you hire us for:

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, optional phone number and message, from our website chat, email or calls.
  • Bookings: details you give when scheduling a call through Calendly.
  • Clients: business contact and billing details, plus any business information, files or account access you share so we can complete your tasks.
  • Website visitors: basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, recorded by our website host and by Google Fonts, which the site uses.

Why we collect it

We use personal information to reply to enquiries, book calls, provide and invoice our services, communicate with clients, keep the records the law requires, and improve our website and services. We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for purposes you would not reasonably expect without asking you first.

Your consent

We collect, use and share your personal information with your consent. When you send us an enquiry, book a call or hire us, you agree to our using your information for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits, by emailing prestovirtuals@gmail.com. We will explain what that means for you, such as no longer being able to receive our services.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the business and deliver your work, such as email and file storage (Google), appointment booking (Calendly), our website host, and the scheduling, invoicing and payment tools we use. They may use it only to provide their service to us. Some of these providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it can be accessed by courts and authorities under local laws. We also disclose information when the law requires it. We never sell your information.

Protection and retention

We protect personal information with password-protected accounts, access limited to the people who need it, and reputable providers. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above: enquiries that do not lead to work for up to 12 months, and client and billing records for as long as we work together plus six years, to meet tax and accounting rules. After that we delete or anonymize it.

Cookies and our website

Our website uses essential cookies and browser storage to work properly, for example to remember that you closed a banner or to run the chat. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add basic analytics, we will tell you here. You can opt out of non-essential cookies at any time from the notice on our site or the Cookie settings link in the footer. Our site links to other sites, such as Calendly, Google and social media, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete, and withdraw your consent. Email prestovirtuals@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. If we cannot give you access, we will tell you why.

Breaches, questions or concerns

If a breach of our security puts your information at real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as feasible, and we keep a record of every breach for at least two years.

To ask a question or raise a concern, email prestovirtuals@gmail.com. If we cannot resolve it, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at 1-800-282-1376.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on our website with its date. This version is effective November 24, 2026.
