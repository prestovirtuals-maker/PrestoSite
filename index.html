FAQ

Good to know

Where do you work with clients?

We're based in Mississauga, Ontario and work remotely with business owners across Canada and the USA, during Eastern Time business hours.

How is pricing worked out?

Administrative support comes in monthly packages of hours (priced the same in CAD or USD): Get Started (40 hours a month, $1,400), Get Organized (80 hours a month, $2,600) and Executive (120 hours a month, $3,600). Social media comes in three monthly plans: Starter ($550), Professional ($950) and Media Boss ($1,450), in CAD or USD (the price is the same in either currency), with optional add-ons at +$150/month each. After your free call you receive a proposal and agreement with the details, or you can have a plan customized to your needs.

How quickly are tasks completed?

Turnaround is usually 2 to 3 business days. For time-sensitive projects, we agree on a deadline before work begins.

How do we communicate?

Email is preferred, since it leaves a timestamped record. Calls are booked in advance through the booking link, and Facebook Messenger is also available. We don't offer on-call support.

Will I work with the same person?

Yes. Building a long-term working relationship and really learning your business is a priority.

Is my information kept confidential?

Confidentiality is a top priority, and an NDA can be signed on request.
