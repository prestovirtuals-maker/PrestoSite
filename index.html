FAQ

Good to know

Where do you work with clients?

We're based in Mississauga, Ontario and work remotely with business owners across Canada, during Eastern Time business hours.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing is a monthly rate based on the number of tasks you delegate, not hours worked. After your free call you receive a proposal with pricing, and you can choose a plan or have one customized.

How quickly are tasks completed?

Turnaround is usually 2 to 3 business days. For time-sensitive projects, we agree on a deadline before work begins.

How do we communicate?

Email is preferred, since it leaves a timestamped record. Calls are booked in advance through the booking link, and Facebook Messenger is also available. We don't offer on-call support.

Will I work with the same person?

Yes. Building a long-term working relationship and really learning your business is a priority.

Is my information kept confidential?

Confidentiality is a top priority, and an NDA can be signed on request.
